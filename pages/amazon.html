The Few Trees That Bring the Amazon Back

The Amazon rainforest 🌳 has thousands of kinds of trees. Sometimes people cut down part of it. The good news is that the forest can grow back.

A Brazilian study 🔬 found that only about 15 to 25 kinds of trees do most of the work. These tough trees can grow in poor soil. They also love the hot sun ☀️ and grow very fast.

Scientists call them pioneer trees. Their leaves 🍃 fall to the ground and make the soil richer. Their shade 🌴 can make the ground up to six degrees cooler. Then other trees can move in.

One helper is the Cecropia, which has a pale trunk and big leaves shaped like hands 🖐️. Another is the Inga, whose long pods hold sweet white fruit you can eat 🍬. Some palm trees help too.

Forests store carbon 🌍 and help keep our planet cool. If we know which trees help the most, people can plant those first. That can bring a forest back faster.

The new forests growing back in the Amazon are already as big as the country of Uruguay 🇺🇾.
