The Giant Glacier of Patagonia

This is a sample story, not the AP article. The Perito Moreno Glacier 🧊 is a huge river of ice in Argentina 🇦🇷.

It sits in the far south, in a region called Patagonia 🏔️. The ice ends in a wall that is taller than a 20-floor building 🏢.

Sometimes giant chunks of ice 🧊 crack off and crash into the lake 💧 below. It makes a loud boom 💥 and a big splash!

Many glaciers 🧊 around the world are shrinking because Earth 🌎 is getting warmer ☀️. Scientists 🔬 watch glaciers closely to learn how fast this is happening.
