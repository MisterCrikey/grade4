Protecting a Special Forest by the Sea

Along the coast of São Paulo, in Brazil 🇧🇷, there is a special kind of forest called restinga 🏖️. It grows in sandy soil close to the sea 🌊. It is part of the Atlantic Forest 🌳.

Restinga is in danger. People want to build homes and hotels near the beach. Very little of it is protected.

Scientists 🔬 studied a place called Bertioga. They found about 1,000 kinds of plants 🌿. Many are orchids 🌺, and 44 kinds of plants could disappear forever.

Many animals live there too. There are lots of frogs 🐸, lizards, birds 🐦 and mammals. Some of them are rare and need help.

The forest also protects rivers 💧. People who live nearby get their water from them.

A group called WWF-Brazil helped make a plan for a new protected area. People talked about the plan at public meetings, so everyone could share ideas.

Original story: WWF-Brasil, February 4, 2010.
