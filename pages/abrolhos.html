Brazil’s Coral Reefs Are in Trouble

Off the coast of Bahia in Brazil 🇧🇷, there is a place called Abrolhos. It has the biggest coral 🪸 reefs in the South Atlantic Ocean 🌊.

Scientists 🔬 watched these reefs for 18 years. They saw the water get very hot 🌡️ again and again. Hot water can make coral turn white. This is called bleaching.

Fire coral 🔥 lives only in Brazil. Now almost all of it is gone. It grows like a tiny tree, and baby fish 🐠 hide in it like a nursery.

Brain coral 🧠 was hurt too. After the very hot year of 2017, almost half of it died.

Dirty water and cutting down trees 🌳 on land can also hurt reefs. Taking care of our oceans helps corals stay alive.
